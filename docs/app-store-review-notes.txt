ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v)) — IN-APP, PERMANENT
Step-by-step for reviewers:
1. Sign in with the review account above (or any account you create for testing).
2. From the main app, open Settings (gear icon on the home screen).
3. In the Account section, tap “Delete account” (red/destructive row, above “Log out”).
4. Read the confirmation dialog, then tap “Delete account” again to confirm.
5. The app deletes the account on our server, shows a success message, and signs you out to the login screen.
6. Optional alternative (same Settings screen): “Request deletion by email” opens the device Mail app with a pre-filled message to info@centifi.app — the primary path is in-app deletion in step 3–5.
